# MF node-selection strategies (Richard's 9514320 "more MF scores") on HPG

Setup: CliqueTrees origin/main @ 9514320 rsynced to /blue/fairbanksj/itaykadosh/gpu/mfstrat/ct (separate copy, env mfstrat/env;
the old rounds/ct-rmain @598b013 was left untouched because earlier results/jobs point at it). Job 47154092, ONE node
c0608a-s25 (hpg-turin, AMD EPYC 9655P, 96 cores), 32 cpus requested, 28 single-thread julia processes in parallel (one per graph; all
8 algorithms of a graph run in the same process, so per-graph ratios share the node). Times are per-process serial (min of 3 for n<1M,
1 run above), but with 28 concurrent processes (memory-bandwidth contention), so absolute times are not comparable to the
milan main_table. Metrics = sbench fillq (nnz(L) incl. diagonal, flops, width = max col count-1; unit weights; isperm asserted).
AMD = CliqueTrees.AMD (AMD.jl), METIS = CliqueTrees.METIS nested dissection, AMF = CliqueTrees.AMF.
Graphs: the 44 of main_table + 3 extra dense TN graphs (rndhyp_T10000, rndhyp_T30000, mc3sat_N3000) = 47; the "TN" family is therefore 4 graphs
(only qaoaamp is in the 44). Families: SS-2D = thermal1/2, parabolic_fem, jnlbrng1, ecology2, Dubcova3, thermomech_TC/dM;
SS-3D/struct = remaining SuiteSparse; circuit = G2/G3; lattice = lat2_*, lat3_*.
Strategies: MF0=F, MF1=F/(d+|K|) baseline, MF2=F/|K| (MMF), MF3=F-d|K| (MIND), MF4=F/sqrt|K|.
W/T/L tolerance +-0.5%. GPU-ND = rounds/nd/results3/r2_t32_b200.tsv, config gndd:256:hyb=1;rmax=4 (28 overlapping graphs).
Raw data: mfs_all.tsv (host tag graph n m alg nnzL flops tw time); tables generated by analyze.py.

## Verdict

- Strategy 2 vs 1 (all 47): nnz(L) -1.3% (29W/3T/15L), flops -2.7% (29/1/17), width -3.8% (33/3/11). A small, not uniform, gain.
- Where it helps: SS-2D (nnz -1.7%, flops -4.4%, 8/8 wins), circuits (-3.3%/-6.9%), TN (-2.1%/-4.7%, width -9%), n>=2M (nnz -6.3%,
  flops -11.8%, 4W/2L). Where it does not: SS-3D/structural (nnz -0.4%, flops -0.5%, 10W/7L, big per-graph swings, e.g. cbuckle +13%, olafu +14%
  nnz; bcsstk17 +6%), and n<500k overall (nnz +0.2..+0.8%, flops +0.7%: a wash). Lattices: -1.6%/-3.1% but 8 losses.
- Not the best single score: strategy 4 (F/sqrt|K|) has the same nnz gain (-1.0%), best on n<50k and lattices; strategy 3 (MIND) gives the
  best width (-5%) and big-graph flops (-7.8% at n>=2M) but loses on nnz/flops on mid-size SuiteSparse (+1.5%/+3%). Strategy 0 is clearly worse (+8% nnz, +19% flops).
  Richard's claim "2 is better" holds weakly on average (about 1-3%), strongly only on 2D/circuit/TN/very large graphs; it is not a universal win.
- Time (28-way parallel, noisy): strategy 2 is 0.93x of strategy 1 overall (0.61x at n>=2M, 1.12x at n<50k, 1.21x on TN dense graphs).
  No real cost; large graphs get faster (fewer/lower-degree updates).
- vs AMD: all MF strategies beat AMD on SuiteSparse/circuits/TN by 10-30% nnz (strategy 2: 0.84x overall, 0.71x SS-3D, 0.71x circuits); on lattices
  MF is ~1-3% WORSE than AMD in nnz (strategy 2: 1.016x). vs METIS-ND: MF is worse overall (strategy 2: 1.205x nnz; 1.23x lattices, 1.32x SS-3D); only 2D/TN are close.
  MF is 4-7x slower than AMD and ~1.9x slower than METIS (TN: much faster than METIS).
- vs our GPU ND hybrid (28 graphs): strategy 2 has 1.325x nnz, 2.03x flops, 1.31x width of GPU-ND (strategy 1: 1.363x/2.14x/1.37x).
  Strategy 2 closes ~3% of the gap; ND still wins big on 3D (lat3_64 1.55x, Serena 1.8x, Geo 2.1x, StocF 2.1x, Bump_2911 2.8x nnz); near parity on 2D (1.0-1.1x), and strategy 2 beats GPU-ND only on ecology2 (0.94x).
